# Certificate Search (Certificate Transparency Log Search)

**Find all SSL/TLS certificates ever issued for a domain and its subdomains**

🔗 Live Tool: https://sslchecktool.com/tools/certificate-search/

---

## 🧠 What It Does

The **Certificate Search** tool lets you query **Certificate Transparency (CT) logs** to discover all SSL/TLS certificates that have been issued for a specific domain and its subdomains. CT logs are public records of certificates issued by Certificate Authorities, and are used to increase transparency and detect mis-issuance or malicious certificates. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## 🔍 What You Can Discover

When you perform a certificate search, the tool can show:

- Every certificate issued for the domain  
- Certificate issuance history over time  
- All subdomains with issued certificates  
- Certificate fingerprints and validity periods

This helps you identify:

- Unexpected certificates issued for your domain  
- Rogue or mis-issued certificates  
- Subdomain coverage and monitoring gaps  :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Enter the **domain name** you want to investigate.  
2. Click **Search Certificates**.  
3. Review the list of certificates associated with the domain and subdomains.  
4. Export or analyze the results to audit certificate issuance and avoid security risks.

---

## 📌 Why It Matters

Certificate Transparency logs are designed to make SSL/TLS issuance public so domain owners, auditors, and security teams can:

- Detect mis-issuance early  
- Monitor certificate lifecycle
- Ensure no unauthorized certificates exist for their domain
- Take action if unexpected certificates appear

CT logging plays a key role in domain security because it helps prevent and expose mistakes or malicious behavior by certificate authorities. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
