## 🧠 What Is an SSL Certificate Checker?

An **SSL Certificate Checker** allows you to verify that your HTTPS-enabled website is secure, trusted, and correctly configured.

The tool analyzes your **SSL certificate details** along with your **TLS configuration**, helping you quickly identify potential security issues or misconfigurations.

---

## 🔍 What This Tool Checks

The SSL Certificate Checker examines:

- **Certificate validity and expiration date**
- **Issuer information and trust chain**
- **Supported TLS protocol versions**
- **Cipher suite configuration and compatibility**
- **Common SSL/TLS misconfigurations**

---

## ✅ What This Helps You Verify

Using this tool, you can confirm that:

- Your SSL certificate is **valid and not expired**
- The certificate is issued by a **trusted Certificate Authority**
- Secure TLS protocols are enabled
- Weak or deprecated ciphers are disabled
- Your site meets modern HTTPS security standards

---

## 🧠 Why Regular SSL Checks Matter

Running routine SSL checks helps you avoid:

- Unexpected downtime caused by expired certificates
- Browser security warnings that reduce user trust
- Weak or outdated TLS configurations
- Compliance and security audit issues

Maintaining a healthy SSL/TLS setup ensures a **secure, reliable, and trusted online presence**.
